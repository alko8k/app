# Recovrd Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Recovrd ("we", "the app") gives you a daily recovery score based on your sleep, activity and habits. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

## Health data

With your permission, Recovrd reads the following from Apple Health:

- Sleep analysis (duration and bedtime)
- Step count
- Active energy burned
- Workouts

This data is used only to calculate your score on your device. **It never leaves your iPhone.** We do not upload it to any server, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or sell it. Health data is never sent to our analytics provider.

You can revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Recovrd.

## Data you enter

Your goals, bedtime and wake targets, training days, reminder times and daily check-ins are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Analytics

We use PostHog to understand how people use the app (for example, which onboarding screens are viewed and whether a trial was started). These events contain no health data, no scores and no personal identifiers such as your name or email. Session recording is disabled.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple; it does not receive your health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

## Children

Recovrd is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
